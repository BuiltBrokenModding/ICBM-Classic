# Ports Policy

## Personal Use

Custom versions are permitted for personal use. Especially when created for personal projects, used locally, or shared with a small group of people. As we believe in the open-source spirit, we encourage users to create their own versions for personal use. Even more find this covered by fair-use as far as we care.

Note, it stops being personal once it is published online. Especially when it is shared with a larger audience or made available for download. If you do this by mistake, don't panic we understand, but we ask that you remove the version from public access.

## Modpacks

Custom versions for modpacks are permitted so long as the scope is limited to the modpack. We require that everything in your power is done to ensure players understand the risks and limitations of using these versions. Along with that, we ask you to note these versions are not created by our team nor supported by our team.

Limited scope means that the modpack is the only thing that uses the version. It is not shared with others or made available for download. Along with the changes are limited to what the modpack needs. Such as integrations between other mods, adjustments to balance, or mechanic changes to match the theme of the pack.

We also recommend that for many changes to just work with our team. As we happily will support improvements, configuration changes, and compatibility support for other mods. In some cases we can help create addons to provide mod support or expand our content.

## Full Ports

Larger ports are more limited. If you fork our project and use just the code, have fun. If you use the assets, we ask that you coordinate with our team. So we can advise on copyrights and publish your version.

Even then we require any fork of our projects to not use our names, likeness, or brands. As this can confuse users and lead to complications.

## AI Ports

AI ports are only permitted for unsupported versions.

Why? We understand users struggle with keeping legacy versions going. While the team is mixed on this, the benefits out weight the negatives. For this we just require coordination with our team. So we can advise how best to share the files without damaging the project.

When you make a port, you are responsible for it. Meaning if the AI breaks the ports, the users are yours. If you fail to maintain the port, we will delist it even if users get upset. As we cannot maintain what we did not create. Not out of lack of understanding but out of lack of commitment. You are effectively on your own if you chose this path. The most we can do is offer advice and share some of our tools.

Even then we may ask that you post an AI port under your own name and project listing. As platforms like Modrith will punish the main project for AI ports. We will do what we can to provide you with access to assets and other critical files for this purpose. So long as you respectfully manage the port. If you do not, we will revoke the license and ask that the project be delisted.

## Recommendation

When making a port... please consider making a new mod inspired by what you love.

We fully support the dreams and asperations of what people make. It's the entire reason the code is open-sourced and free to use for any means. You can use it to make an entire new project, expand an existing project, make addons, or even your own fun twists on our projects. As this is the best way to expand the community and bring joy to those that love modding.

That said, do keep in mind the assets are mixed license. So while some are free to use, others are limited and may not be usable. Each artist has their own views on copyright, and we ask that you respect their wishes. If you find yourself needing something similar, you are free to be inspired. We can even provide tips and advice on how to create your own versions.
