# Pull Request Policy

Contributions are welcome as long as they follow our general policies and are respectful of the project.

## General guidance

* Give us a heads-up before starting
* Small PRs are quick to review and accept
* Larger PRs are hard to review and will likely be rejected
* Work needs to be your own and align with our vision
* Assets need a chain of custody to show how it was sourced, license, and attribution

## Fixes

Small fixes are easily accepted. Overhauls or system changes need to go through our team. We are happy to talk over your plans to align with the team's vision.

For a fix to be accepted, it should be small and focused on a specific issue. We need some level of documentation on the issue and how the fix addresses it. We also recommend providing a test case to verify the fix. Along with a step-by-step guide on how to reproduce the issue. The more you can provide, the quicker the PR is reviewed and accepted. More doesn't mean write a novel. Keep the PR description short and to the point.

## Mod updates

For the most part, the team handles updating for newer versions. If you have a specific need or want to contribute, please reach out to the team for guidance. We can provide access to push directly to branches to work towards a port. Along with giving direction on how best to work with the project.
